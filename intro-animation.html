How to Animate in Canva

Quick 2-minute setup for Instagram Reels & Web Intros

1
Create Video in Canva: Open Canva → Select Video (1920×1080) or Mobile Video (1080×1920).
2
Upload Asset: Drag & drop images/logo/abha-logo-primary-mockup.jpg or the downloaded video from this page.
3
Add Canva Animation: Select image → Click Animate on top bar → Choose "Breathe" or "Pan" with 3-second duration.
4
Add Golden Shimmer Overlay: Go to Elements → Search gold sparkles overlay → Place on top with 40% transparency.
5
Export: Click Share → Download → MP4 Video (or GIF).
