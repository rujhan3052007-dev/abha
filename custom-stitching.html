Precision Guidelines

Simple Body Measurement Guide

Keep the measuring tape comfortable—not too tight, not too loose.

1. Bust Circumference

Across the fullest part of bust

Place the tape under your arms and measure around the fullest part of your chest. Make sure the tape is flat across your back.

2. Natural Waist

At your narrowest torso curve

Measure around your natural waistline, usually located an inch above your belly button where your body bends side to side.

3. Hip Circumference

Around the widest hip point

Stand with feet together and measure around the fullest part of your hips and seat to ensure ease of sitting and movement.

4. Kurta Length

Shoulder to desired hemline

From the top of your shoulder (near the collarbone) down past the knee to your preferred hem length (typically 40" to 44").

5. Sleeve Length

Shoulder seam to cuff

Measure from your outer shoulder point down to where you want the sleeve to end: short (6"), elbow (11"), 3/4th (16"), or full (20").

6. Bottom Style

Salwar, Straight Pant, or Churidar

Select your preferred comfort style. Classic pleated salwar offers rich volume, straight pants offer sleek modern lines, and churidar provides heritage gathers.
