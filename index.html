Step 1 of 5

Bust / Chest Measurement

Important Fitting Rule: Please enter your actual body measurements, not the size of an existing stitched suit. ABHA master tailors add standard ease and 2-inch inside margins automatically.
💡 How to measure your bust?

Wrap the measuring tape around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the tape level across your back and comfortable enough to breathe normally.

💡 How to measure your waist?

Measure around your natural waistline, typically an inch above your belly button or where you tie your salwar/pants.

💡 How to measure your hip?

Stand with feet together and measure around the widest part of your hips and rear.

💡 How to measure kurta length?

Measure straight down from the highest point of your shoulder (near the neck) to your desired hemline (knee or below knee). Standard length is 40–44 inches.
