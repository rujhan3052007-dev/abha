About ABHA

ABHA was founded on a simple yet transformative belief: that Indian ethnic clothing should never force a compromise between exceptional fabric quality and a tailored, personal fit. While traditional stores sell unstitched materials and leave the search for a master tailor to the customer, ABHA unites premium loom curation with master tailoring expertise under one roof.

From authentic handloom Chanderi silks and breezy summer Jaipur cottons to rich festive suits and traditional Bandhani prints, every Salwar Suit at ABHA can be delivered unstitched or handcrafted to your exact anatomical measurements.
