About ABHA

Connecting authentic Indian dress materials, personalized tailoring craftsmanship, and friendly local service from Beawar, Rajasthan.

Who We Are

ABHA is a retail clothing and dress-material business rooted in Beawar, Rajasthan. We specialize in authentic Indian salwar suit materials and bespoke tailoring services, bridging traditional Rajasthani textile artistry with modern e-commerce convenience.

The heart of ABHA is simple: giving customers the freedom to purchase unstitched dress materials exactly as woven, or have them custom stitched to their own measurements by experienced master tailors.

"Your Style, Your Measurements, Our Experience, Your ABHA."

Our Key Pillars

Authentic Dress Materials

Pure breathable cottons, traditional Rajasthani Leheriya, hand-tied Bandhani, and festive Chanderi silk. No artificial filters or color alterations.

Personalized Tailoring Atelier

Master Tailors in Beawar handle cutting, stitching, and finishing according to precision customer measurements.

Physical Store & Online

Customers can order online with doorstep delivery or walk into our flagship boutique at 11, Ganesha Tower in Beawar.

Customer-First Care

From tailor guidance on WhatsApp to a 10-day complimentary fit alteration window on custom stitched garments.
