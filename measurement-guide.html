Salwar Suit Measurement Guide

Learn how to take accurate body measurements or measure your best-fitting salwar suit. Keep the measuring tape comfortable and not pulled too tight.

🔄 Interactive Measurement Unit Converter

1 Inch = 2.54 CM
1

Kurta Length

Place the tape at the highest point of the shoulder (near neck base) and measure straight down over the chest to your desired hemline (knee, calf, or ankle).

Standard: 40 – 44 Inches
2

Shoulder Width

Measure from the tip of one shoulder bone across the natural curve of your upper back to the tip of the other shoulder bone.

Standard: 13.5 – 15.5 Inches
3

Bust / Chest

Wrap the tape around the fullest part of your chest with your regular undergarments on. Ensure tape is level across your back.

Standard: 32 – 44 Inches
4

Waist

Measure around your natural waistline, typically about 1 inch above the navel or where your body naturally indents when bending sideways.

Standard: 28 – 40 Inches
5

Hips

Measure around the widest part of your hips and buttocks with feet together. Keep the measuring tape parallel to the floor.

Standard: 36 – 48 Inches
6

Sleeve Length

From the top tip of your shoulder down your arm to your desired sleeve length (e.g., 6" for short, 17" for 3/4th, 21" for full).

Most Popular: 17 Inches (3/4th)
7

Armhole

Measure around your shoulder joint through the armpit with your arm relaxed at your side. Add 1 inch for comfortable arm movement.

Standard: 15 – 19 Inches
8

Bottom / Salwar Length

Measure from where you tie your salwar or pants waist down to the ankle bone or top of your shoes.

Standard: 37 – 40 Inches
