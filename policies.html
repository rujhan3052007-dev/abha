Shipping & Delivery Policy

1. Beawar Local Doorstep Delivery (PIN 305901)

Because our flagship boutique is situated right at 11, Ganesha Tower in Beawar, Rajasthan, we provide direct, personalized doorstep delivery within Beawar city:

  • Unstitched Dress Materials: Processed immediately and delivered to your doorstep within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Bespoke Stitched Suits: Tailored by our master artisans and delivered within 3 to 5 days upon completion.
  • Delivery Charges: Free delivery is available on qualifying orders above our store threshold (managed dynamically from store administration). For orders below the threshold, a standard local fee applies.

2. Pan-India Express Courier Shipping

For orders across Rajasthan and the rest of India, we partner with reliable express logistics providers including Blue Dart, Delhivery, DTDC, and India Post.

  • Dispatched with a unique tracking code sent via SMS & WhatsApp.
  • Estimated transit time: 3 to 7 business days from dispatch date.

3. Delivery Address Verification

Please ensure that your complete address and active contact number are provided accurately during checkout. For high-value handcrafted fabrics, our delivery personnel or courier may require a one-time OTP or signature upon handover.

Order Cancellation Policy

Important Rule for Stitched Suits: Stitched orders can be cancelled only before fabric cutting and bespoke tailoring commences. Once cutting begins, the fabric has been irrevocably customized and cancellation is no longer possible.

1. Unstitched Orders

You may cancel an order for unstitched fabric at any time prior to shipping dispatch. Once dispatched, standard return/exchange terms apply upon delivery.

2. Stitched Orders

Because custom stitching involves individual master cutting to your unique measurements, cancellation is governed strictly by the production stage:

  • Before Cutting Begins: Full 100% refund initiated to your original payment method.
  • After Fabric Cutting / In Tailoring: Non-cancellable, as the cut material cannot be restored to original dress material roll.

3. How to Request Cancellation

To request a cancellation, please immediately contact our store support via phone or WhatsApp at +91 98290 XXXXX or email support@abha.store with your Order ID.

Alteration & Return Policy

1. Bespoke Stitched Garments — 10-Day Alteration Window

Custom-stitched garments are tailored exclusively to individual body measurements and cannot be returned for cash refund. However, our commitment to your perfect fit is uncompromising:

  • Generous Seam Allowances: Every ABHA stitched suit is crafted with 1.5-inch inner fabric margins on both sides to facilitate effortless alteration if your body measurements fluctuate.
  • Complimentary Alteration: We provide free alterations within 10 days of receiving your garment. You can bring it directly to our Beawar store or courier it back for adjustments.

2. Unstitched Dress Materials — 7-Day Return/Exchange

Unstitched dress materials may be returned or exchanged within 7 days of delivery, provided that:

  • The fabric has NOT been cut, stitched, altered, washed, or pressed.
  • All original tags, packaging, and labels remain intact.
  • The piece is free from perfume, stains, or handling damage.

3. Damaged or Incorrect Items

If you receive a package that is visibly tampered with or damaged in transit, please notify us within 48 hours with photographic evidence. We will arrange a replacement or full refund promptly.

  • Single-Piece Dedication: When a 1-of-1 item is ordered, it is immediately reserved for that customer.
