Answers to Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the exact fabric cuts provided in an unstitched suit? ↓
Every unstitched suit material includes generous 3-piece cuts: Top (Kurta) 2.50 Metres, Bottom (Salwar/Pant/Churidar) 2.50 Metres, and Dupatta/Shawl 2.50 Metres. This ensures adequate fabric for plus sizes, full-length kurtas, and pleated Patiala bottoms without compromising drape.
How does online custom tailoring work at ABHA? ↓
When ordering any salwar suit, choose "Custom Stitched to My Measurements". You can provide your measurements (Bust, Waist, Hip, Length, Bottom style) through our interactive Measurement Studio on the product page, or send photos/measurements of your best-fitting kurta directly to our master tailor on WhatsApp (+91 9214837104).
What if the stitched suit needs slight fitting adjustments? ↓
Every custom-stitched suit crafted at ABHA includes 2 full inches of inside seam margins on both sides. If you ever want to loosen or tighten the fit, any local tailor or you yourself can easily open or adjust the inner seam in 2 minutes.
What are the dispatch timelines and delivery charges? ↓
We provide 100% Free Pan-India Delivery on all orders. Unstitched suit materials are dispatched within 24 to 48 hours. Custom stitched suits undergo hand cutting, interlock sewing, quality check, and pressing, taking 4 to 6 business days before express dispatch.
Can I place my entire order directly on WhatsApp? ↓
Yes, absolutely! Many of our patrons across India prefer ordering via WhatsApp. Simply click the "Order on WhatsApp" button on any product or shopping bag page, or message us at +91 9214837104 with the suit screenshot. We'll assist with measurements and payment details directly.
Are the woollen suits warm enough for North Indian winters? ↓
Yes! Our Woollen and Pashmina-blend suits are specifically woven for winter comfort. The kurta fabric retains body warmth while staying lightweight, and the accompanying 2.5m woollen shawl provides full cozy coverage for chilly evenings and festive winter gatherings.
