Stitching & Customization

Unstitched Dress Material: You receive the complete set of premium cut fabric (Kurta material, Bottom/Salwar material, and Dupatta) ready to be tailored by your personal tailor. Unstitched orders ship within 24 to 48 business hours.

Stitched by ABHA: Our master artisans in Beawar meticulously cut, line, and tailor the suit to your exact measurements, neckline, sleeve length, and bottom style. Custom stitching adds ₹450 to ₹650 depending on the style and takes approximately 3 to 5 tailoring days.

We craft three signature silhouettes:

  • Straight Cut Kurta & Pant: Elegant, contemporary, and versatile for everyday office and casual wear. Includes side pockets.
  • A-Line Kurta & Palazzo: Flowing, relaxed silhouette providing maximum comfort and graceful drape.
  • Traditional Salwar Kameez: Classic pleated Patiala or semi-Patiala salwar paired with a traditional cut kameez.

You can also specify your preferred neck design (Round, V-neck, Boat neck, or Mandarin collar) and sleeve length (Sleeveless, Elbow-length, or 3/4th).

Alterations & Cancellations

Unstitched Orders: You can cancel your unstitched order at any time before it has been dispatched from our store for a 100% refund.

Stitched Orders: You can cancel your stitched order as long as our master tailor has not yet started cutting the fabric. Once fabric cutting or bespoke tailoring begins, the order cannot be cancelled because the fabric is cut exclusively to your individual specifications.

We take pride in our precision tailoring. All ABHA stitched suits include generous 1.5-inch inner fabric margins on both sides to allow effortless size adjustments.

We provide complimentary alteration service within 10 days of delivery. You can bring the suit to our Beawar store or contact our support team at +91 98290 XXXXX to arrange adjustment.
